# Inline credit history — performance and owner UX

The owner-approved single combined Cards root displays purchase/payment history and credit statements directly below the selected card. There is no mandatory drilldown for viewing a credit card's activities.

Initial view renders 8 source-order movements and 4 statement cycles. Accessible in-place controls incrementally reveal further canonical rows, preserving IDs, exact amount values, owner/card boundaries and privacy mode. Full-row actions open the existing shared event read details. Saveable progressive disclosure resets when the active canonical card ID changes, and responsive vertical arrangement supports large fonts.

No backend/API/schema mutation, auto SMS capture, local canonical duplicate database or release/signing changes.

The owner also placed the net-position breakdown in Reports rather than the everyday account list. Reports now provides a canonical drilldown that returns to its originating Reports stack; the Home-origin Accounts list no longer presents this report card.
